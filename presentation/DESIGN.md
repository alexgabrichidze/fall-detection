# Fall Detection — hackathon / science fair deck

Audience: project judges and technically curious visitors. Target: approximately
five minutes, ten widescreen slides, English. Evidence snapshot: application
commit 1d72e63. Detection evaluation is pending; all demonstration inputs are
explicitly simulated.

## Visual direction

An engineering field notebook with editorial scale. Deep evergreen-charcoal,
warm ivory, mint emphasis, restrained terracotta for pending work. Space Grotesk
Bold headings, DM Sans body copy, tabular-feeling numerical displays. Camera
focus brackets recur as a structural motif. Native editable diagrams and text;
no stock photos, invented medical statistics, or implied accuracy figures.

## Slide plan

1. **Cover.** Oversized title left; abstract body-landmark diagram framed on the
   right. Dark background. Establish the privacy-first prototype.
2. **Problem / question.** Large question occupies the upper left. A three-part
   response gap runs along the bottom. Warm ivory background; open composition.
3. **Concept.** Four stages span the width, with cancellation visibly branching
   below the countdown. Dark background. Label this the intended complete flow.
4. **Privacy architecture.** Broad local-computer region; small external Telegram
   destination at right. Only text crosses the boundary. Ivory background.
5. **Vision pipeline.** Vertical sequence on the left, a large model-compatibility
   gate on the right. Ivory with terracotta accent. Entire pipeline marked pending.
6. **Safety logic.** State progression across the center, large incident numeral
   at left, two invariants underneath. Dark background. Mark application behavior
   implemented and tested using simulated evidence.
7. **Demo.** Large response-window interaction schematic on the left; three short
   operator actions on the right. Mint background. Explicitly simulated, not a
   screenshot or live detection result.
8. **Evidence.** Oversized 43 at left; three test categories at right. Warm ivory
   background. State that these are software tests, not detection accuracy.
9. **Next experiment.** Three numbered stages, arranged as a stepped sequence:
   checkpoint compatibility, representative clips, real hardware/delivery. Ivory
   with terracotta markers. Outcomes to record, not invented measurements.
10. **Close.** A large two-line thesis at left, implemented/pending summary at
    lower right, and clear prototype limitation. Dark background.

Layouts alternate between asymmetrical cover, open question, horizontal process,
boundary diagram, verification gate, state machine, demo, evidence, staged roadmap,
and typographic close. Text blocks use explicit hierarchy and generous spacing.

## Sources and caveats

Content derives from AGENTS.md, IMPLEMENTATION.md, README.md, application code,
and the observed 43-test run. No external epidemiology or market claims are used.
43 tests passing establishes tested application behavior only. Live camera/model
compatibility, detection performance, prerecorded prompt playback, and Telegram
end-to-end delivery remain unverified. Raspberry Pi is outside current scope.
